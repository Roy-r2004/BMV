# Film site: implementation plan

**Goal:** build the white, cinematic site Roy approved in the preview
(https://claude.ai/artifact/ExNTPGfFny9QmUNdV63aC6) into the React frontend, keeping the
firm's footer (`SiteFooter`) and every working part of the current pages.

**Approved by Roy, 2026-11-07:** "ok apply these change but ofc ensure to have our footer".

## What the preview fixes

- White stage; one field of ink and cobalt light (three.js points) behind every public page.
- Each page is a scene, each section a shot; a shot's `data-shape` re-forms the field.
- Page changes are film cuts: letterbox bars close to a slit with the scene's title, the
  camera pushes in, the bars open on the new page. An opening title plays once per session.
- Headlines rise word by word; a burst between shapes; far dust in depth; scroll dolly;
  grain; breathing glow; cursor ring; magnetic buttons; slate with timecode and progress;
  shot rail on the right.
- The BMV logo in the bar, the footer and the chat button.

## Constraints

- Facts only: wording comes from the current pages and `data/` files. No invented clients,
  figures or names. $200 consultation.
- Keep `SiteFooter` content and links; restyle it only.
- Out of scope: `/demo` (the consultation app), engagements, results, admin, which keep their
  own layouts.
- `prefers-reduced-motion`: no cut, no burst, no dolly, no rising titles.
- Without WebGL the pages still read and work.
- Checks: `npx tsc -b --noEmit`, `npm run lint` (baseline 0 errors), `npx vite build`;
  screenshots at 1440 and 390.

## Structure

- `src/film/shapes.ts`: shape generators (points + colours), as in the preview.
- `src/film/field.ts`: the light field (main points, dust, burst, flows, pointer, dolly).
- `src/film/FilmShell.tsx`: layout route for public pages: field, nav, rail, slate, cut,
  intro, cursor, grain, outlet (held during a cut), `SiteFooter`.
- `src/film/FilmNav.tsx`: the bar and the phone curtain menu, with the auth states of `SiteNav`.
- `src/film/Shot.tsx`, `src/film/Rise.tsx`: a shot section; a rising headline (`*word*` = emphasis).
- `src/film/scenes.ts`: route to scene number and title; which paths are film paths.
- `src/styles/film.css`.
- Pages rebuilt as shots: home, services, approach, examples, private AI, about, contact,
  sign in, sign up; privacy and terms through `FirmPage` / `PageHero` / `Section`.
- Removed once unused: `productScene.ts`, `OperationField.tsx`, `EnvironmentModel.tsx`,
  `ConceptScreens` (if unused elsewhere), `landing.css`, `about.css`, `private-ai.css`,
  `examples.css`, `Team.tsx`.
